QUESTION 4: Top 10 users who got retweeted the most from other users
Explanation: Extracted the foreign key (ReferencedTweet id) from retweets in file 1, joined with file 2 on tweet ID to look up the original author, and counted occurrences of the original author.
Commands:
- Key extraction: grep -h "type=retweeted" downloaded_tweets_extend_nolf.ASCII.CLEAN.tsv | sed -n 's/.*ReferencedTweet id=\([0-9]*\).*/\1/p' | tr -d '\r' | sort > key1file.sorted.txt
- Author lookup table: cut -f1,2 downloaded_tweets_extend_original_ids_nolf.ASCII.CLEAN.tsv | tr -d '\r' | sort -k1,1 > tweetfile2.sorted.tsv
- Join & Count: join -t $'\t' -1 1 -2 1 key1file.sorted.txt tweetfile2.sorted.tsv | cut -f2 | sort | uniq -c | sort -nr | head -n 10
